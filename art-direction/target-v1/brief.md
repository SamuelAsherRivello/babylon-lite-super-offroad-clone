## Prioritized Blender direction
1. Preserve landscape full-course readability first. Establish the drivable loop, wide lanes, all landings, and free sightlines in graybox before decorating. Camera approximately 55 degrees above ground; orthographic extent tuned to include the entire terrain and edge margin.
2. Make trucks compact boxes with selective bevels, oversized separate wheels, a short pickup bed, bold saturated paint and visible contrasting roof numbers. No logo or branded profile. Use the brief's 3.2 m initial truck length, not measurements inferred from this image.
3. Build broad low dirt berms, a clear tabletop and shallow washboard. Maintain shared authored gameplay geometry rather than copying the image's approximate junctions.
4. Use tan/ochre earth, dark brown mud, restrained sandstone facets and cool shadow/fill. Broad colors and geometry should carry the style; texture scratch/noise is optional and should be much lower than in the concept.
5. Use tire groups and low fences selectively around safety boundaries, a simple gantry, only one small spectator structure and three reusable rock variants. Keep dust trails small and transient.
6. Use bright blue nitro and green traction pickups but place them on legal drivable surfaces; do not reproduce the off-course blue canister in the concept.

## Conflicts and visible defects
Status: needs-revision as literal production reference; usable as an art-direction guide when corrected through the approved prompt.
- The concept includes extra parked utility vehicles, heavy machinery, pipes, dense vegetation and elaborate surrounding cliff scenery. These exceed the asset brief; omit them.
- Its terrain and tire repetition are more detailed than the performance-conscious intended assets. Simplify rather than chase photographic parity.
- The blue pickup is on an inaccessible rock platform, conflicting with collection gameplay. Use a legal lane.
- The image gives limited safety margin at the right edge and some outer terrain is cropped. Production view must include the full course with comfortable margin.
- Track/shortcut junctions and checkpoint legality are ambiguous in a generated image. Resolve against the authored track definition; do not infer exact lap topology.
- Number forms are approximate; author precise digits and flat contrast in the game.
- The concept does not prove four-wheel pivots, collision geometry, suspension feasibility, or the absence of occlusion at all gameplay positions.

## Proportions, hidden forms, and budget assumptions
Arena remains initially approximately 80 × 60 m; vehicle approximately 3.2 m long; passing lanes about three truck widths where practical. These come from the approved prompt, not image metrology.
